# Ores by depth  [REFERENCE, Counsel 2026-10-31, for the mineralogy talk]

David asked for the ores and minerals of Minecraft, Vintage Story and Dwarf Fortress side by side,
ranked by a reasonable mean depth: "making sure that areas are diverse, but viable, will require
careful balancing."

**The graphic:** https://claude.ai/artifact/U8GUqssppWJwT7p16gjgZg (private to David). It has the
ranked depth log, the full cross-reference table and the balancing notes. Its source is in Counsel's
scratchpad, so this page keeps the list itself.

**Scale:** about 130 blocks of crust, the scale all three share:
- Minecraft: from a surface near Y 64 down to Y −64;
- Vintage Story: from a surface near Y 120 down to bedrock;
- Dwarf Fortress: roughly 100–150 z-levels down to the magma sea.

The depths are Counsel's proposals, for David to change. They're best stored as a share of the crust,
since how deep the world goes is still open (`vision/watch-list.md`).

## Balancing, in short

- **Viable:** every starting region has within reach:
  - fuel (wood, then coal);
  - copper and tin, or iron;
  - flux (limestone);
  - salt;
  - flint or stone.

  A map readout of the distance to the nearest of each would show any gaps. In Dwarf Fortress,
  players hunt for flux and iron in the embark finder.
- **Diverse:** ores tied to fields the placer will offer:
  - rivers: placers;
  - coasts: mineral sands;
  - deserts: evaporites;
  - hot, wet land: bauxite;
  - wetlands: bog iron;
  - mountains: lode gold, emeralds, tin;
  - basalt: native copper, amethyst, sulfur;
  - basins: coal, phosphorite, banded iron;
  - rare points: diamond pipes, craters.
- **Asks of the placer:**
  - depth below the surface as a field;
  - rock type as a field;
  - rivers, rain, temperature and crush;
  - point fields;
  - child deposits (gold inside quartz, silver inside galena, sylvite inside salt).
