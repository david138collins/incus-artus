# The journal: contents

How we got here. `master/` holds what's true now; this folder holds the record: David's decisions in his own words,
the experiments, the reviews, the proposals. Add to it; never rewrite it. Corrections go in the open.

Kept by Counsel. Whoever adds a file here adds its line below.

---

## `docs/`: the decisions and Builder's records

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `decisions.md` | Every decision, in David's words where he gave them, dated, with its reason, tagged [DECIDED], [OPEN] or [LATER]. The first place to look for why something is the way it is. |
| `experiments.md` | Each experiment and measured step: its pass mark, written before it flew, then the results and the logs that hold them. |

## `vision/`: Counsel's

Edited only when David asks.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `handoff.md` | Short notes between sessions. Read it at the start of a session; clear a note once it's acted on. |
| `oracle-return.md` | Counsel's brief for the Oracle's next call: what we've learned since its last review, and our questions. |
| `oracle-review.md` | The Oracle's reviews. The one file the Oracle writes. |
