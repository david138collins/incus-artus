# <Project>: decisions

Every design and technical decision we've agreed, in David's words where he gave them, dated, with its reason
beside it. Added to, never rewritten: a change of mind is a new entry that says what it replaces. Older entries are
how we got here; `master/` is what holds now, and an old word weighs less than a recent one.

Status tags: **[DECIDED]** agreed · **[OPEN]** still needs an answer · **[LATER]** noted, not built yet

How an entry reads:

- **[DECIDED <date>] <What was decided, in a few words>** (David, <to whom, or answering what>: "<his words>").
  <What it means in practice.> Why: <the reason, so a later reader, David included, knows why>.
