# Handoff: notes between sessions

Builder and Counsel can't see each other's conversations. They message each other directly (find each other with
ListAgents), and anything that should outlast the moment goes here or in `master/`.

**How to write a note:** newest at the top, dated, signed **Builder** or **Counsel**, a few lines. Say what changed
and what the other one should do about it, if anything. When a note has been acted on, the reader marks it *(seen)*;
clear it once it's done with. Anything decided belongs in `journal/docs/decisions.md`, not here.
